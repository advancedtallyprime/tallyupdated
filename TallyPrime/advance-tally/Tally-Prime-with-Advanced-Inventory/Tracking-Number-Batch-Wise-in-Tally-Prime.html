Core Concepts: Tracking Number vs Batch Number

Advanced inventory management mein goods ki movement aur lot traceability ko maintain karne ke liye do powerful tracking tools use hote hain:

  • Tracking Number: Ek unique reference jo inventory movement (inward/outward shipments) ko specific orders, delivery notes, ya receipt notes ke saath link karta hai.
  • Batch Number: Stock ke particular lot ya manufacturing group ko identify karta hai jisme expiry dates, manufacturing dates, aur quality parameters tied hote hain (essential for Pharma, FMCG, Chemicals).

Comparison Matrix

Particular Tracking Number Batch Number
Main Focus Transaction & shipment movement tracking Lot-wise stock & shelf-life quality control
Quantity Tracking Links movement quantity to billing Tracks physical stock divided across lots
Expiry Date Support Not primary purpose Crucial (Mfg Date & Expiry Date)
Issuance Logic Reference linkage FIFO or FEFO (First Expiry, First Out)
Primary Industry Use General trading, logistics, distribution Pharmaceuticals, Food & Beverage, FMCG
Golden Inventory Rule:
Tracking Number batata hai ki maal kahan se aaya aur kahan gaya (Movement Reference).
Batch Number batata hai ki stock ka kaunsa manufacturing lot hai aur uski expiry kab hai (Quality & Lot Control).

Part C: Expiry Management & FEFO Concept

FEFO (First Expiry, First Out):
Pharma aur consumable industries mein jis batch ki expiry date sabse pehle aati hai, us stock ko pehle issue/sell kiya jata hai taaki expiration loss se bacha ja sake.

Verification Reports, Common Mistakes & FAQs

Verification Reports in TallyPrime:

  • Stock Summary & Batch Summary (Alt + G → Batch Summary): View lot-wise available quantities, manufacturing dates, and exact expiration timelines.
  • Stock Expiry Report: Track items nearing expiry or already expired to prevent obsolete stock dispatch.
  • Pending Bills / Tracking Reports: Reconcile movement references against financial invoices.

Common Mistakes:

  • Creating separate stock items for each batch (e.g., Face Wash B-001, Face Wash B-002) instead of maintaining a single item with active batch allocations.
  • Failing to link Sales Returns back to their correct original batch, breaking lot traceability.
  • Ignoring expiry dates during sales fulfillment in perishable inventory categories.
  • Confusing Tracking Numbers (movement references) with Batch Numbers (lot identities).

Frequently Asked Questions:

  • Q: Batch Number aur Tracking Number mein main difference kya hai?
    A: Tracking Number shipment/movement reference ko link karta hai, jabki Batch Number physical stock ke manufacturing lot aur expiry ko identify karta hai.
  • Q: Kya ek hi stock item ke multiple active batches ho sakte hain?
    A: Haan, business multiple shipments mein alag-alag batches receive kar sakta hai aur saare active batches parallelly maintain hote hain.
  • Q: FEFO kya hota hai?
    A: First Expiry, First Out — jis batch ki expiry date sabse pehle ho, use pehle sale/issue karna.
